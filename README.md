**ENGINEERING PROJECT REPORT PAPER-BASED LED               CHRISTMAS GREETING**   
 **1\. Aim**  
           To design and develop a paper-based LED Christmas greeting card using conductive copper tape, an LED and a coin-cell battery, and to demonstrate the operation of a simple closed electrical circuit.  
 **2\. Objectives**   
          • To understand the basic operation of a DC electrical circuit. • To use copper tape as an electrical conducting path. • To operate an LED using a compact coin-cell battery. • To integrate electronic components into a greeting-card design. • To develop a simple, low-cost and portable electronic prototype**.**  
 **3\. Introduction**   
          A paper-based LED greeting card is a compact application of basic electrical and electronic engineering principles. The electrical circuit is integrated into a paper card so that the conducting paths become part of the physical design. A coin-cell battery provides the DC supply, conductive copper tape forms the interconnections, and an LED provides the visible outputCARD

**4\. Components Required**  
  A4 paper / Card paper

* Red colour paper  
* Pencil  
* Ruler  
* Scissors  
* Colour pens  

**5\. working**

     1\. The coin-cell battery acts as the DC source.

 2\. Copper tape carries the electrical connection from the battery to the LED.

 3\. The LED is placed with correct polarity.

 4\. When the conducting path is completed, current flows through the LED.

 5\. The LED emits visible light and creates the required Christmas-card lighting effect.

 6\. Opening the circuit stops current flow and turns the LED off.
